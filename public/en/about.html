About Zayamara

The Path That Connects Us.

Who we are

Zayamara is a tourism, culture and experiences brand from Mozambique. The name was created from the sound of Bantu languages and expresses a path that unites those who travel with those who welcome.

The logo brings together a golden sun, a flowing path and the waves of the Indian Ocean.

Mission

Connect travellers with people, places, cultures and authentic experiences, valuing local guides and communities.

Vision

To be an African reference in cultural tourism, from Mozambique to the whole continent.

Values

  • AuthenticityShowing Africa as it is, told by those who live it.
  • Human connectionEvery journey begins and ends with people.
  • RespectFor communities, traditions and nature.
  • DiscoveryCuriosity as a way of travelling.
  • ResponsibilityTourism that leaves benefits where it goes.
  • African prideCelebrating the continent's cultural diversity.

Objectives

  • Give visibility to local guides and their stories.
  • Create cultural, gastronomic, nature and heritage experiences.
  • Establish Mozambique as the starting point.
  • Grow progressively into other African countries.

Want to be part of this path?

Traveller, local guide or partner: tell us what you are looking for and we will reply personally.
